## Meet Dain

The letters from Saju Letter carry a voice named Dain. **Dain is a fictional character created for Saju Letter — not a real person** — a persona built to give the letters a consistent voice.

Here's Dain's backstory: someone who spent years working an ordinary office job before starting to study Four Pillars reading seriously, and found real joy in helping people notice the quiet rhythms of their days. That's the voice behind the letter you get each morning.

## Not a mystical gift — just a backstory of years of study

There's no special power or inherited talent in this story. Just a warm, steady voice that hopes to be a small presence in your day.

Saju Letter isn't meant to feel like a fortune teller's pronouncement — it's meant to feel like hearing from someone familiar, morning after morning. That's why every letter comes from one consistent voice instead of a different persona each day: warm on a hard day, quietly encouraging on a good one — the kind of voice you get to know over time.
